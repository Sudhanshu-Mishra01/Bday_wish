# 💖 Romantic Birthday Surprise Web App

An interactive, multi-stage birthday adventure created to celebrate and cherish your girlfriend on her special day.

## ✨ Highlights
- **Act I: The Vintage Wax-Sealed Envelope** (Interactive seal crack & letter slide-out)
- **Act II: Constellation of Love Crystals** (Interactive click hunt revealing 6 romantic reasons)
- **Act III: 3D Polaroid Memory Lane + Golden Scratch Card** (Flippable photos & canvas scratch reveal)
- **Act IV: The Heartfelt Love Letter Scroll** (Long poetic paragraphs, gilded drop caps, love reaction tokens)
- **Act V: Interactive Cake Cutting Ceremony** (Tap to blow flickering candle flames + golden knife slicing)
- **Act VI: Cinema Birthday Wish Video & Keepsakes** (Video screening, fireworks, certificate generator, WhatsApp share)
- **Built-in Procedural Music Synthesizer** (Web Audio API romantic chimes & arpeggios, no external mp3 needed)
- **In-App Customizer Panel** (`⚙️` button) to personalize names, photos, notes, and video without writing code

## 🚀 Quick Start
```bash
python3 -m http.server 3333
# Open http://localhost:3333 in your browser
```
